## Editing Notes

| Segment | What to show | What to cut |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Voice input "What is Gemini 3" | Full recording | - |
| Slideshow generation | 2s of loader | ~25s wait |
| Slideshow playback | Slide 1 full speed, rest at 2x | - |
| Mystery Lab | Briefing + crime scene scan | Witness/timeline/warrant |
| Wonder Lab | Prediction cards + 1 reveal | Results summary |
| Story Studio | 1 chapter + illustration | Remaining chapters |
| Constellation | Full pan, click Gemini 3 star | - |
| Simple level demo | Voice + 1-2 simpler slides | Generation wait |
| Chinese demo | Voice + 1-2 Chinese slides | Generation wait |

## Recording Tips

- Record each segment as a separate take for clean editing
- Keep mouse movements slow and deliberate — fast clicking looks chaotic on video
- Use a clean browser window (no bookmarks bar, no other tabs)
- Pre-populate 5-8 topics in the constellation before recording
- Record voiceover separately for cleaner audio (don't narrate live)
